    What this twin does and does not do

    • Sizes are yours to set: type them in the request or use the size form. A rule checks they make sense and fit the 300 mm build plate.
    • Claude reads the request and picks the part, material, a tested parameter set, the layer height, interpass temperature and machining allowance. It never writes coordinates.
    • Fixed rules (R01–R08) check every plan; a refusal goes back to Claude with the rule and the reason, and it re-plans.
    • A deterministic slicer turns the plan into layers and bead paths: outline then infill on the pad, concentric beads on walls, starts staggered and direction alternated layer to layer.
    • The beads cover the CAD shape plus the machining allowance plus 0.5 mm waviness, because WAAM is near-net shape and is finished by machining.
    • No melt-pool, distortion or residual-stress physics: the heat model is one lumped temperature, for showing the interpass rule at work.
    • The robot is a simple six-axis arm with the torch held vertical; reach is checked with the same arm.
    • The rules are a process check, not a certified safety function.
    • If the planning server is unreachable or its demo limit is reached, a rule-based planner fills in, and the log says so.
